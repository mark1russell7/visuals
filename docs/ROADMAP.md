# Roadmap

This file is the plan of visuals. The Status page of the site reads it, and the tracker of the project mirrors it.

| ID | Item | Status | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | The stage core | done | `@mark1russell7/visuals` has a stage on three.js: a camera that a person moves, views by name, picking, highlights, themes and a picture only after a change. The camera maths is pure, so a test in Node.js examines it. The stage comes from the viewer of `@mark1russell7/room-view`. Version 0.1.0 is on npm. |
| R2 | The globe | in progress | A content for a stage that shows the Earth: from the whole Earth to one region, with areas and points as data. Version 0.1.1 has it in the entry `/globe`: surfaces that follow the curve of the Earth, outlines, a grid, styles by kind, picking and a filter. The sphere maths is in `@mark1russell7/geo` 0.5.0. The home page of the site shows it with sample data. In version 0.2.3, the surface at the middle of the picture follows the pointer in a drag, at each height of the camera. In version 0.2.4, a view of regions fills the width or the height of the picture, and `stageSupported` tells a page if it loads the stage. In version 0.3.1, a style with `dash` draws the edge of its areas as a dashed line. Next: the place under the pointer. |
| R3 | The time scrubber | in progress | A control that replays how records change with time, for each stage. Version 0.2.1 has it in the entry `/scrubber`, with the style sheet `/scrubber.css`: a slider on an axis with a tick at each change, a button for the replay and a button for the speed. The model of a replay is in the root entry and needs no browser. The control writes no inline style, so a strict content security policy permits it. Next: the scrubber in the alerts command view, on the history of real records. |
| R4 | Marks | in progress | The marks that each viewer draws: a point, a run, a link, a field and a meter, each with a level. Version 0.2.0 has them as plain data in the entry `/marks`, with the shape of each level and `checkMarks`. The entry imports no three.js, so a package with no browser gives marks. The state of the pointer and of the selection has the new name highlight. In version 0.3.0, a meter can have no limit: the first add-on of room-view found this gap. Version 0.3.2 has the entry `/markers`: a content that draws the marks on a stage. A sign looks at the camera, a run is a round line, a link is a dashed line and a field is a grid of colors. A mark behind a solid stays in the picture with less color, and the pointer finds a sign there. A pick of a stack can have a distance, so the nearest record of two contents gets the pointer. The home page has a sample room with each mark. Version 0.3.3 has `transformMarks`: the marks of one space in a different space, for example from a room with the z axis up to a stage with the y axis up. Next: the value of the cell of a field below the pointer, and the marks in the viewer of room-view. |
| R5 | The room viewer on the stage | open | `@mark1russell7/room-view` builds its viewer on the stage, and keeps only the model of a room. |
| R6 | The demo site | in progress | The site becomes a live demo: a stage with sample data that a person turns, selects and replays. The home page has a live globe with sample warnings and earthquakes: a list that selects a record, a legend that filters, and three views. A browser with no WebGL 2 gets a flat map. The scrubber replays three days of the sample: the areas come, change their level, move and go. The home page has also a conversation and its topics on the flat stage, where a person gives a part its topic. Next: the marks of a room on a stage. |
| R7 | The labels | in progress | The names of the places of a stage, as texts of the page. Version 0.2.2 has them in the entry `/labels`, with the style sheet `/labels.css`. Each text is next to its point, off each other text and off each mark. `placeLabels` is the pure function of the places. `stackContents` shows the layer with the content that it names. The home page names the place of each earthquake. Next: the names of the areas on the home page, with `bboxCenter` of `@mark1russell7/geo` 0.5.2. |
| R8 | The flat picture | in progress | A picture of boxes and edges that a person moves and zooms, for example a graph of topics. The entry `/graph` is the home of its drawing: the boxes, the routes and the labels that the layout engines give. It imports no three.js and no engine. Version 0.3.4 has the maths of its camera: a zoom at a point, a pinch, the free area of a picture, the fit of a group of boxes and a move between two views. In a move, each point of the picture goes along a straight line. The gestures of the pointers and the actions of the keys are pure functions too. Version 0.3.5 has the stage on a canvas: a drag, the wheel, two fingers, the keys, the picks, the views as aims and the layers of a content. A part of the picture stays in view, and a person can show all of a very large picture. Version 0.3.6 has the drawing: `createPictureContent` shows a picture of `@mark1russell7/layout-contract` 0.10.4, with its boxes, its routes and its labels. The looks give the colors, the lines and the signs of each record from its state. The selected record and the records at it stay clear, and each other record has less color. A text that is too small to read becomes bars. The content makes shapes only for the records in view. Version 0.3.7 has the names in a far view: a label look with `least` keeps the height of a line on the screen, and two such texts do not overlap. In `plainLooks`, the name of a box with children stays a text until the box is small on the screen. Version 0.3.8 has a focus that follows the lines in view: a route that the looks do not show brings no box into the focus. In version 0.3.9, a name of a far view that an edge of the view cuts moves into the view. The home page of the site has the first view on this stage: a conversation and its topics, with made-up data. An engine places the boxes with springs in the shape of the view, a router gives the lines, and a card shows the topics of each part. In a document of many proposals, a proposal gets its line when a person shows it. Next: a move between two layouts. |
